## Notes

- Engine first (T-01 to T-05): every rule is pure TypeScript with the random generator and time injected.
- e2e flows depend on the seed deep link (T-13): flow values (balls of seed 42, a winning row) are generated by a
  Jest test from the engine and fail on drift.
- Risk: text-to-speech availability differs per phone (no French voice installed); the app must stay silent and
  stable without it (AC-21). Android 11+ needs the TTS_SERVICE package query: checked in T-15 on the prebuilt
  manifest.
- Risk: animation smoothness on a mid-range phone; Reanimated runs on the UI thread, 90 board cells are static Views.
- AC-31 (nicknames) and AC-32 (feel) are judged by the user on the phone during /release-apk.
